# ShopEase Customer Support Policies

Note: ShopEase is a fictional company created for the VoxAssist AI demonstration project. These policies are sample data, not the policies of a real company.

## 1. Shipping and Delivery

ShopEase offers standard shipping with an estimated delivery time of 3–5 business days after dispatch.

Express shipping is estimated to take 1–2 business days after dispatch.

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed and may be affected by weather, holidays, or courier delays.

Customers receive a tracking number after their orders are dispatched.

## 2. Returns

Customers can request a return within 7 calendar days of delivery.

Items must be unused, undamaged, and returned with their original packaging and included accessories.

Some items may be ineligible for return for hygiene or safety reasons. The eligibility of a specific product must be confirmed by customer support.

Customers should contact support with their order ID to initiate a return.

## 3. Refunds

Refunds are processed after the returned item has been received and inspected.

Once approved, refunds are generally initiated within 5–7 business days.

The time taken for the money to appear in a customer's account may depend on their bank or payment provider.

If a refund has not arrived after the expected processing period, customers should contact support with their order ID.

## 4. Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation before their order is dispatched.

Orders that have already been dispatched may not be cancellable through the standard cancellation process.

Customers should contact support with their order ID to check cancellation eligibility.

A cancellation request is not considered confirmed until the company verifies it.

## 5. Payment Methods

ShopEase accepts credit cards, debit cards, and supported UPI payment methods.

Payment availability may vary depending on the customer's location and payment provider.

If a payment fails, customers should verify their payment details and check whether their bank or payment provider reported a failed transaction.

Customers should not share their passwords, PINs, or OTPs with customer support.

## 6. Order Tracking

Customers can track an order using the tracking number provided after dispatch.

If tracking information is unavailable immediately after dispatch, customers should allow up to 24 hours for the courier system to update.

If tracking remains unavailable after 24 hours, customers should contact support with their order ID.

VoxAssist must not invent tracking numbers, order statuses, or delivery dates.

## 7. Damaged or Incorrect Items

Customers who receive a damaged or incorrect item should contact support with their order ID and a description of the problem.

Photographs may be requested to help verify the issue.

Replacement or refund eligibility must be confirmed by customer support. VoxAssist must not promise a replacement or refund before approval.

## 8. Human Support

Customers may request a human support representative at any time.

Issues involving disputed payments, exceptions to return policies, unconfirmed refunds, and complex order problems should be escalated to human support when the knowledge base does not provide a definitive answer.

VoxAssist must clearly explain when it cannot verify information or complete an action.
